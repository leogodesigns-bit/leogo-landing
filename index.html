About

Six years of brands. One of them our own.

Leogo Designs is run by Shweta Phansalkar from Satara and Pune. We started with logos and Instagram, and learnt how Indian shoppers really buy by growing Shweta's food page, Shweta Eats, to 26,000 followers: in DMs, not on websites.

When our own jewellery brand, Ikaa, outgrew Shopify and a pile of apps, we built the tools it needed. Those tools are now the stack. Studio work is remote across India, except social media management, which we do for brands in Satara and Pune.
